Disney’s *Frozen JR* adapts the story of sisters Elsa and Anna for young casts while keeping the emotional spine intact: fear, love, and the courage to be seen. Elsa is the vocal and dramatic center of many productions — “Let It Go” and the character’s isolation ask for power, vulnerability, and control. Even in a JR edition, Elsa must feel regal and human, not merely a karaoke turn.

Elyse portrayed Elsa at The Grand Theater, carrying a leading musical theatre track with iconic audience expectations. Roles like Elsa test nerves as much as technique: spectators know the song, so the actor’s job is to make the moment character-true again. That means breath management, storytelling through lyric, and staying connected to Anna and the company so the spectacle serves the sister relationship.

This credit is casting-relevant for young leading / ingénue musical tracks that need belt, emotional clarity, and the poise to hold a house that arrives already humming the score.
