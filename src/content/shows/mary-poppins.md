*Mary Poppins* brings P.L. Travers’s magical nanny to the stage with a score full of dance breaks, chimney sweeps, and precise company storytelling. The dance ensemble builds Cherry Tree Lane and the park; a dance captain sits one step further into leadership — cleaning combinations, running notes, and keeping the company consistent across a long performance stretch.

Elyse served as dance captain at Rome Little Theater in the spring, through a three-week performance run. That assignment asks for more than learning choreography once: she had to hold the show’s movement standard night after night, support fellow dancers, and stay vocally and physically reliable while still contributing onstage. Classic family musicals reward precision and goodwill; the company must look joyful on the twelfth performance as clearly as on opening.

For casting, dance captain credits signal rehearsal leadership, choreographic literacy, and stamina — useful proof that Elyse can elevate company dance work and help maintain a production after tech is over.
