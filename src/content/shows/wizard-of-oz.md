*The Wizard of Oz* remains one of theatre’s most familiar journeys: Kansas dust, a cyclone, and a road toward courage, heart, and home. Ensemble members populate Munchkinland, Oz, and the shifting worlds between — often with quick costume changes, character switches, and vocal parts that keep the fairy-tale engine running. Without a strong company, Dorothy’s adventure has no world to travel through.

Elyse performed in the ensemble at The Grand Theater, supporting a large classic musical with the kind of reliable company work that makes stars look good. Ensemble in *Oz* is a master class in transformation: one night you are a Munchkin, a flying-monkey energy, a citizen of the Emerald City — always storytelling, never “just filling space.” The craft is in the details: focus, spacing, and selling every picture even when you are not the one holding the ruby slippers.

This credit, stacked with her featured Grand Theater roles, shows a performer who understands both ends of the cast list and treats ensemble responsibility as craft, not a placeholder between “real” parts.
