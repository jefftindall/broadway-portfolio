John Cariani’s *Almost, Maine* is a romantic comedy of interlocking short plays set in a fictional northern town where love arrives like weather — sudden, strange, and oddly precise. The evening mixes whimsy and ache: lost shoes, broken hearts made literal, and characters who say more through stillness than speech. Hope is one of those human anchors in the quilt of scenes, requiring sincerity that never tips into sentimentality.

Elyse played Hope at Cartersville High School, working inside a play (not a musical) where timing, listening, and emotional honesty carry the night. Without a score to lean on, the actor’s instrument is text and partnership. Hope’s scenes reward clarity of objective and a light touch — comedy that stays grounded in character truth.

This credit broadens Elyse’s casting profile beyond musicals: she can play contemporary comic drama, hold silence, and serve an ensemble evening of vignettes where every short play must feel complete.
