*Annie* follows an optimistic orphan who softens a hard-edged billionaire and reminds a Depression-era New York that hope can be stubborn. The orphanage girls are the show’s comic and musical engine in the early scenes — harmony, mischief, and the chaos of “Hard Knock Life.” Molly is a featured child track with personality: she is not Annie, but she helps define the world Annie escapes and returns to with new purpose.
