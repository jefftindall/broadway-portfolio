Beatriz, a spirited undocumented Mexican woman, hasn’t seen her daughter—16-year-old Olivia—in years. When Olivia’s blog reveals hints of suicidal thoughts, Beatriz appears at her doorstep insisting on a seven-day cross-country road trip. Between tension and reconnection, their shared experiences spark healing and a deeper mother-daughter bond.
