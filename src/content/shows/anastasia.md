*Anastasia* draws on the legend of Grand Duchess Anastasia Romanov: an amnesiac young woman, a city of secrets, and a musical that moves between intimacy and spectacle. Lily is the witty, fashionable friend whose timing and warmth open doors — socially and emotionally — around Anya’s journey. The role lives in sparkle and specificity: clear storytelling, comic precision, and vocal presence inside a large ensemble world.

Elyse portrayed Lily at Marietta’s historic Strand Theater (July 8–10), bringing comic musical-theatre strengths to a professional regional stage. Lily is not a background ornament; she shapes scenes through listening, banter, and the kind of generous scene partnership that makes leading tracks land. Elyse’s work emphasized character-first humor — laughs that come from relationship and status, not from mugging — while meeting the musical’s stylistic demands for clarity and brightness.

This credit matters for casting directors seeking an ingénue/comic musical actress who can hold focus in a classic Broadway-style score, stay connected in group numbers, and deliver polished, audience-ready comedy in a short, high-pressure performance run.
