Anastasia is a musical inspired by the legend of Grand Duchess Anastasia Romanov. Set in the aftermath of the Russian Revolution, it follows a young amnesiac named Anya who sets out to discover her past. Elyse portrayed Lily in the Strand Theater production (July 8–10, 2025).
