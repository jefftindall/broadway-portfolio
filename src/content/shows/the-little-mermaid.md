In May 2026, Elyse took on the iconic role of Ursula in Woodland High School's production of *The Little Mermaid*, an official entry evaluated by the Georgia High School Musical Theatre Awards (Shuler Award) committee.

Stepping away from traditional Broadway vibrato, Elyse brought her own distinct, modern flair to the role with a bold, pop-forward vocal performance. The high-energy production featured full digital backdrops and a wildly talented ensemble cast.
