Disney’s *The Little Mermaid* follows Ariel’s longing for the human world and the sea-witch bargain that reshapes her fate. Ursula is the story’s engine of temptation and power — a comic villain whose vocals, physical comedy, and command of the stage have to land as entertainment and threat at once.

Elyse played Ursula in Woodland High School’s April 2026 production, an official entry evaluated by the Georgia High School Musical Theatre Awards (Shuler Award) committee. Rather than defaulting to traditional Broadway vibrato alone, she shaped a pop-forward vocal approach that kept the character contemporary while protecting stamina across the score’s belt and character work. The role asks for more than “evil laugh” theatrics: Ursula sells a deal, owns the room, and still has to listen in scenes with Ariel and the ensemble so the comedy stays character-true.

For casting, this credit shows Elyse’s comfort with larger-than-life musical antagonists, CCM-leaning vocal color when the production calls for it, and the discipline to carry a high-visibility track in a fully staged, adjudicated high-school musical with digital scenery and a large ensemble around her.
