*Listen*, written and directed by West Hyler for the TYA/USA conference hosted by the Alliance Theatre, splits one story into three storylines that the audience follows through silent-disco headphones. The production played in the Farideh & Al Azadi Gallery; the performance rewinds twice so viewers can re-enter the mystery from a new channel — an immersive form that asks the company to stay precise across overlapping timelines while the house chooses which thread to track.

Elyse’s assignment sits at the intersection of acting and live musicianship: holding a character inside Hyler’s headphone-driven structure, staying present for partners across split narratives, and anchoring musical texture from the keyboard without pulling focus from the storylines the audience is unlocking.

For casting, *Listen* is contemporary devised-adjacent theatre with a named director, a national conference context, and proof that Elyse can work in nontraditional formats — gallery architecture, silent-disco tech, and multi-channel storytelling — while still delivering a clear character track from the piano.
