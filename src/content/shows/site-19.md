*Site 19* is a film project directed by Don Stallings and produced by Atlanta Workshop Players and Juice Factory Media (July 2026). Shot and celebrated with a red-carpet premiere in July, the production brought together a company of young performers for a contemporary screen story — the kind of local film set where actors, musicians, and directors work in close collaboration rather than in siloed departments.

Elyse was cast as a background actress and also collaborated with the directors to create music for the film and coach fellow actors. That dual footprint matters: background work asks for focus, continuity awareness, and the discipline to serve the frame without pulling focus, while music collaboration and on-set coaching draw on her vocal pedagogy and CCM strengths to support the cast’s sound and performance. Few credits combine on-camera presence with creative partnership behind the scenes in the same production.

For casting and industry readers, *Site 19* expands Elyse’s profile beyond the stage — film set experience, collaborative musicianship, and the ability to coach peers while still showing up as a reliable company member in front of the camera.
