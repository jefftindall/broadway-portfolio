*Little Women* follows the March sisters as they grow up amid Civil War-era pressures, artistic ambition, and family loyalty. Amy begins as the youngest — vain, sharp, hungry for beauty and status — and matures into an artist who still wants love and belonging. The musical asks Amy to travel from comic impatience to genuine vulnerability without losing the character’s spark.

Elyse portrayed Amy at Cartersville High School, a featured sister track that carries substantial book scenes and musical moments. Amy is easy to play as a caricature; the richer choice is specificity — status games with Jo, tenderness with Beth, and a growing seriousness about art and identity. Elyse’s approach favored character-driven comedy and clear storytelling so audiences could enjoy Amy’s foibles while still rooting for her growth.

Casting-wise, Amy demonstrates ingénue/young leading range with comic timing, sisterhood chemistry, and the stamina to sustain a named role across a full musical evening.
