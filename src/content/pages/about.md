## Background

Elyse Tindall is a musical theatre actress from Atlanta, Georgia, now based in New York City for school and training. She is an alumna of the Broadway Artists Alliance and has trained at the University of the Arts. Her credits include *Anastasia* (Lily), *Miss You Like Hell*, *Almost, Maine*, *Little Women*, industry showcases, and off-Broadway cabarets — including her NYC debut at Ripley-Grier Studios.

## Philosophy

I believe theatre is about honesty under lights — finding the specific human truth in every scene, then sharing it generously with the audience. Whether I’m playing comedy or heartbreak, I work from curiosity, discipline, and joy. The goal is never to “perform at” people; it’s to invite them in.

As I grow as an artist in New York, I’m committed to craft over shortcuts: listening partners, precise text work, healthy vocal technique, and showing up prepared so directors and collaborators can trust me in the room.
